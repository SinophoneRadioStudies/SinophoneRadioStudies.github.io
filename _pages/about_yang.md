### Research Assistant: Rui Yang 杨睿

**Email: <u3642567@connect.hku.hk>; <yangrui560@gmail.com>**

Rui Yang received a bachelor's degree in Electronic Information Engineering from CUHK-Shenzhen and an MA in Chinese Historical Studies from HKU. She is interested in modern Chinese history, the history of science and technology, digital humanities, and AI-assisted programming. Tools and platforms she has built are collected at [ray-bot-ai.github.io](https://ray-bot-ai.github.io), including a [full-text search of the Shanghai Municipal Police Files, 1894–1949](https://ray-bot-ai.github.io/smp-files/).

In the "Sinophone Radio Studies" project, she plans to devise an OCR pipeline, a full-text search function, an AI search agent and other features.
